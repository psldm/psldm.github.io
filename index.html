About

An independent researcher, systems architect and entrepreneur based in Los Angeles. I work in mathematics, theoretical physics, software development and AI.

I design software and computing infrastructure, develop computational models and build AI tools. My technical work uses Rust, C/C++ and Linux. It covers system architecture, implementation, debugging and testing.

I founded and ran a network security company. My experience includes product development, production processes, team management and business operations.

Selected work

  1. The Theory of Temporal Spheres

    I proposed a finite-universe hypothesis with radius R = cτ and Poincaré dodecahedral topology, then examined its predictions for the Hubble tension, the CMB quadrupole and the absence of matched circles in Planck data.

  2. Beyond the 32: Superconducting Pairing Channels of the Icosahedral Group — Classification, Approximant Branching, and Ginzburg–Landau Theory for Quasicrystals

    I classified superconducting pairing channels for icosahedral quasicrystals, calculated their Ginzburg–Landau invariants, and built exact-arithmetic code that regenerates the paper’s tables and equations.

  3. The Roman Dodecahedron as a Sexagesimal Computing Device: Group-Theoretic Structure, Physical Encoding, and Computational Signatures in Kepler and Ptolemy

    With John Pirs, I investigated the hypothesis that the Roman dodecahedron was a hand-held base-60 computing device, mapping its 12 apertures, 20 knobs and 60 rotational states onto A5 and reconstructing historical calculations step by step.
