One object runs through all of it. The regular dodecahedron, its rotation group A5 of order 60, and the binary icosahedral group 2I of order 120.

The same structure surfaces in the topology of the cosmic microwave background, in the factorization of base 60, in the exceptional Lie algebra E8, and in a bronze artifact left behind by Roman surveyors. Each appearance is documented on its own terms, without forcing a single theory over them.
